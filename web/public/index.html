W forward. S back. A/D turn. F floor shot. Space aim + shoot. T offers a truce. G proclaims that you are loaded.




Rules

2 to 4 players. Last one alive wins.


You hold up to 2 bullets, but you only need one to kill.


Space starts aiming. A moment later the shot goes off straight the way you face. You can't move while aiming, and everyone sees your red aim line.


Closer = better odds. Point blank always lands, far away it's more up to chance. A miss still costs the bullet, so be careful.


3+ players: face someone and press T to offer a truce. If they face you and press T back, you're allies and share vision. Shoot your ally and you're a traitor, everyone sees you for 15 seconds. Truces end when only 2 are left.


G says "I'm loaded" in the feed. Could be true. Could be a bluff.


Every 10 seconds the player with the most kills gets pinged on everyone's map.


A floor shot wastes a bullet but proves you're armed. At 2 you stay armed. At 1 you just emptied yourself.


A quiet player is either empty or holding. Rush them or don't.


Every shot is heard. A ! shows where it came from, even through walls.


After 60 seconds the walls close in, one ring every 15 seconds. Get caught in one and you die.






Legend

Minimap: green arrow is you, red arrows are enemies, cyan is your ally, pink is a traitor.


Gold diamonds are ammo. White rings are gunshots you heard, red rings are bounty pings.


Dark spots are scorch marks from floor shots. Black squares are fog: you can't see there.






Rooms

Create room gives you a code. Send it to friends, they type it and hit Join. You press Start.
