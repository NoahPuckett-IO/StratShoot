WASD move and turn. F floor shot. Space shoot the way you face.




Rules

2 to 4 players. Last one alive wins.


You hold up to 2 bullets. Nobody sees your ammo but you. One shot kills.


Shots fly straight the way you face. A miss still costs the bullet.


A floor shot wastes a bullet but proves you're armed. At 2 you stay armed. At 1 you just emptied yourself.


A quiet player is either empty or holding. Rush them or don't.


Every shot is heard. A ! shows where it came from, even through walls.


After 60 seconds the walls close in, one ring every 15 seconds. Get caught in one and you die.






Legend

@ you. Letters are other players. Arrows show where someone faces.


o ammo. X dead. ! gunshot heard. % * , scorch marks, fresh to old.


Blank space is fog: you can't see it.






Rooms

Type any code, like BEANS, and tell your friends the same code. The first one in presses Start.
